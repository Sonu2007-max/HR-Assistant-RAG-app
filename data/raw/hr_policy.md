# Employee Handbook & HR Policy Guidelines
Company: TechCorp Global Enterprises
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

## 1. Leave Policies & Accrual Rules
- **Earned Leave (EL) / Privileged Leave:** Employees accrue 1.5 days of EL per month, totaling 18 days per calendar year. EL can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days. Encashment of EL is permitted only upon resignation or retirement, capped at 15 days.
- **Casual Leave (CL):** Employees are entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave per year, credited on January 1st. CL cannot be carried forward and lapses if unused by December 31st. Consecutive CL cannot exceed 3 days without prior manager approval.
- **Sick Leave (SL):** 10 days of paid Sick Leave are provided annually. For sick leaves exceeding 3 consecutive working days, submission of a registered medical practitioner's certificate is mandatory upon return to work.
- **Maternity Leave:** Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children. This leave can commence up to 8 weeks prior to the expected delivery date.
- **Paternity Leave:** Male employees receive 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be availed within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

---

## 2. Office Working Hours & Remote Work Policy
- **Core Hours:** Standard office timings are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
- **Hybrid Work Model:** Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to team lead coordination and core meeting attendance requirements.
- **Overtime & Compensatory Off:** Employees required to work on weekends or company holidays for critical deployments are eligible for Compensatory Off (Comp-Off), which must be utilized within 60 days of accrual.
