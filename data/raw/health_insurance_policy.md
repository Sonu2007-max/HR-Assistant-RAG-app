# Comprehensive Employee Health Insurance & Medical Benefits Guide
Plan Provider: Apex Health Assurance Corp
Policy Tier: Comprehensive Corporate Gold

## 1. Coverage Limits & Premium Structure
- **Sum Insured:** ₹5,00,000 (5 Lakhs) per employee family floater per annum (covers Self, Spouse, up to 2 Children, and Dependent Parents).
- **Room Rent Limit:** Standard private room up to ₹5,000 per day. ICU charges are covered on actuals without capping.
- **Co-pay:** 10% co-pay applicable for claims originating in Tier-1 metro hospitals for non-emergency elective procedures; 0% co-pay for network hospitals and emergency admissions.

---

## 2. In-Patient Hospitalization & Pre/Post Care
- **Pre-Hospitalization:** Medical expenses incurred 30 days prior to hospital admission (diagnostic tests, physician consultations) are fully covered under reimbursement.
- **Post-Hospitalization:** Follow-up treatments and prescribed medicines for 60 days post-discharge are covered up to 10% of the sum insured.
- **Daycare Procedures:** Over 140 daycare treatments (e.g., cataract surgery, dialysis, chemotherapy) that do not require 24-hour hospitalization are fully covered.

---

## 3. Exclusions & Waiting Periods
- **Pre-Existing Diseases (PED):** Covered after a waiting period of 24 consecutive months of continuous employment with TechCorp Global.
- **Dental & Cosmetic Treatments:** Routine dental checkups, teeth whitening, and cosmetic surgeries are strictly excluded unless necessitated by accidental facial trauma.
- **Maternity Expenses:** Covered up to ₹50,000 for normal delivery and ₹75,000 for cesarean delivery after a waiting period of 18 months.

---

## 4. Network Hospitals & Emergency Claims
- **Cashless Facilities:** Employees can avail cashless hospitalization across 4,500+ network hospitals pan-India by presenting the Apex Health E-Card and company ID.
- **Reimbursement Claims:** For non-network hospital admissions, original itemized bills, discharge summaries, and diagnostic reports must be submitted via the HR portal within 30 days of discharge for settlement.
